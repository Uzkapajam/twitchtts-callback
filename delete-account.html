UzkaChat Account and Data Deletion

UzkaChat uses Twitch OAuth authentication and does not create separate native user accounts.

How to Remove Your Account Access

  1. Open Twitch Settings
  2. Go to Connections
  3. Revoke UzkaChat (TTS (Uzkapajam)) access
  4. Uninstall the app from your device

Local Data Deletion

To remove locally stored app data, uninstall the app or clear app storage from your device settings.

Data Retention

UzkaChat does not maintain separate long-term user accounts. Any local authentication tokens remain on your device unless removed by the user.
