UzkaSpeak Account and Data Deletion

UzkaSpeak uses Twitch OAuth authentication and does not create separate native user accounts.

How to Remove Your Account Access

  1. Open your Twitch account settings.
  2. Go to Connections.
  3. Revoke the Twitch connection used for UzkaSpeak.
  4. Uninstall the app from your device.

Local Data Deletion

To remove locally stored app data, uninstall the app or clear app storage from your device settings.

Data Retention

UzkaSpeak does not maintain separate long-term user accounts. Any local authentication tokens remain on your device unless removed by the user.
